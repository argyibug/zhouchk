Chengkang Zhou

Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Zi Yang Meng. My research centers on dynamical spectra in quantum magnetism, integrating large-scale quantum Monte Carlo simulations, stochastic analytic continuation, and quasiparticle approaches including linear spin wave and Schwinger boson theories to uncover collective excitations and phase-transition signatures in magnetic and topological quantum materials. Representative outcomes include identifying the amplitude (Higgs) mode via dimensional crossover [Phys. Rev. Lett. 126, 227201 (2021)], revealing dynamical signatures of fracton topological order [Phys. Rev. Research 4, 033111 (2022)], and collaborating with experimental teams to interpret spectroscopic observations in candidate quantum spin liquids [Nat. Phys. 20, 1097-1102 (2024)]. I am passionate about advancing our understanding of quantum materials and fostering collaborations across theory and experiment.

Feel free to reach out to me if you'd like to discuss research or explore potential collaboration!
